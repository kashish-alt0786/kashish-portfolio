About Me

Researching trustworthy Artificial Intelligence for Healthcare.

Who am I?

I am an aspiring Medical Information Technology researcher from India with a strong interest in Explainable Artificial Intelligence, Healthcare Machine Learning, and Digital Health.

My research focuses on developing AI systems that clinicians can trust. Rather than building models with only high accuracy, I believe medical AI must also provide transparent explanations for every prediction.

My current work includes developing NutriGuard AI, an educational nutrition analysis system for diabetes management, and an Explainable AI Diabetes Risk Prediction model using XGBoost and SHAP.
